# Smart Triage

**Description**

Smart Triage is an AI-powered emergency healthcare application designed to provide quick and organized support during medical emergencies.
It connects patients, ambulance drivers, and hospitals through a single platform. 
The application provides SOS emergency assistance, location sharing, ambulance support, AI-assisted injury assessment,
first-aid guidance, hospital dashboards, and patient prioritization based on emergency severity.
It also includes doctor assignment, emergency notifications, multilingual support, and women's health monitoring. By combining AI, emergency communication,
location services, and healthcare management, Smart Triage aims to improve coordination and support faster response during critical situations.

**1. Objectives**

* To provide quick and efficient assistance during medical emergencies.
* To help prioritize patients based on the severity of their condition.
* To connect patients, ambulance drivers, and hospitals through a single platform.
* To provide AI-assisted injury assessment and first-aid guidance.
* To improve communication and coordination between emergency responders and hospitals.
* To provide health monitoring and support features for patients.
* To reduce delays in emergency response and help healthcare providers manage critical cases efficiently.

**2. Key Features**

* 🚨 **SOS Emergency Assistance** – Allows users to request emergency help quickly.
* 🚑 **Ambulance Support** – Helps connect patients with ambulance services and provides location information.
* 📍 **Location Tracking** – Supports location sharing for emergency response and ambulance tracking.
* 🩺 **AI Injury Assessment** – Provides AI-assisted information about injuries and possible first-aid steps.
* 🏥 **Hospital Dashboard** – Allows hospitals to view and manage incoming emergency cases.
* 🔴🟡 **Patient Prioritization** – Classifies emergency cases based on urgency.
* 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Assignment** – Helps hospitals assign doctors to incoming patients.
* 🔔 **Emergency Notifications** – Provides important alerts to users and emergency responders.
* 👩 **Women's Health Monitoring** – Supports health tracking and provides relevant health guidance.
* 🌐 **Multilingual Support** – Makes the application accessible to users speaking different languages.
* 🤖 **AI Healthcare Assistance** – Uses AI to provide healthcare-related information and assistance.
